Privacy Policy

Effective: October 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how NOIR processes information when used on Discord. Information processed depends on the features enabled and the permissions granted to the bot.

1. Information We Process

Depending on usage, NOIR may process Discord user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, server configuration, command information, voice-state events, and moderation-related information needed to operate its features.

2. Message Content

NOIR may process message content when required for prefix commands or enabled features such as link filtering and anti-spam protection. Do not assume that all features operate without accessing message content. Any storage of message content depends on the bot's actual implementation and enabled features.

3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to execute commands, maintain server settings, operate moderation and voice protection features, diagnose errors, prevent abuse, and meet legal obligations.

4. Storage and Retention

NOIR may store configuration and identifiers needed to maintain features between restarts. Retention depends on the information, feature, and actual storage implementation. Information should be deleted when no longer necessary, subject to applicable legal requirements.

5. Third-Party Services

NOIR relies on Discord and may use a third-party hosting provider. These providers process information as needed to provide their services and are subject to their own policies. We do not sell personal information or share it for third-party advertising.

6. Security

Reasonable measures should be used to protect stored information. However, no internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security. Never share passwords or authentication codes with NOIR.

7. Data Requests and Deletion

Users and server administrators may contact the developer to request access to, correction of, or deletion of relevant information, where applicable. Requests will be reviewed subject to legal requirements and technical limitations.

Removing NOIR from a server may stop further processing but may not automatically remove previously stored configuration. Additional deletion may be necessary.

8. Children's Privacy

Users must comply with Discord's age requirements and applicable laws. NOIR is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

9. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated when NOIR's features, data practices, or legal obligations change. The latest version will be published at this URL.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact the NOIR developer through your official support server or published contact method.

NOIR is an independent Discord application and is not affiliated with Discord Inc.
